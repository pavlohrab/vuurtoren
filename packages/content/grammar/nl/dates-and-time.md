## Explanation

### The clock

- Full hours: _Het is drie uur._ (3:00)
- _kwart over drie_ (3:15), _kwart voor vier_ (3:45).
- **half** points to the **next** hour: _half vier_ = 3:30 (half way to four).
- Minutes around the half hour count from _half_: _vijf voor half vier_ (3:25), _vijf over half vier_ (3:35), _tien over half vier_ (3:40).
- Minutes near the full hour count from the hour: _tien over drie_ (3:10), _tien voor vier_ (3:50).
- Timetables use the 24-hour clock: _om 15.30 uur_, said _vijftien uur dertig_.

"At three" is _om drie uur_. "What time is it?" is _Hoe laat is het?_

### Days, months, dates

Days and months take a **small letter**: _maandag, dinsdag, woensdag, donderdag, vrijdag, zaterdag, zondag_; _januari, februari, maart, april, mei, juni, juli, augustus, september, oktober, november, december_.

Dates put the **day first**: _op 5 mei_ (spoken _vijf mei_), _maandag 3 juni 2024_. Short: _3-6-2024_ or _3/6_ = 3 June.

### Little time words

- _op maandag_ (on Monday), _'s maandags_ (on Mondays), _in juni_, _in 2020_, _om acht uur_.
- _'s ochtends / 's morgens_ (in the morning), _'s middags_, _'s avonds_, _'s nachts_.
- _vandaag, morgen, gisteren, overmorgen, eergisteren_; _volgende week_, _vorig jaar_.
- Age: _Ik ben dertig (jaar)._ _Hoe oud ben je?_

### Pattern

- `half` + next hour → _half acht_ = 7:30
- `[minutes] voor/over half` + next hour → _vijf over half acht_ = 7:35
- date: `[day] [month] [year]` → _12 maart 2025_
- `om` + clock time, `op` + day/date, `in` + month/year/season

## Examples

- Hoe laat is het? Het is **half negen**. | What time is it? It is half past eight.
- De les begint **om kwart over tien**. | The lesson starts at a quarter past ten.
- Ik ben jarig **op 14 februari**. | My birthday is on the 14th of February.
- **'s Zondags** slapen we lang uit. | On Sundays we sleep in.
- De trein vertrekt **om tien over half zes**. | The train leaves at twenty to six.
- We gaan **in augustus** op vakantie. | We are going on holiday in August.

## Common mistakes

- _Half acht_ for 8:30. Half points forward: 8:30 is _half negen_.
- Capital letters: _Maandag_ in the middle of a sentence. Write _maandag_.
- _In maandag_ or _op juni_. Days take _op_, months take _in_.
- Month first: _mei 5_. Say _5 mei_.
