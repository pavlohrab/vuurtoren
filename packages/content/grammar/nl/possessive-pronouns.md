## Explanation

A possessive word stands before a noun and says whose it is: _mijn fiets_ (my bike). Most have a full form and a short spoken form.

**ons** or **onze**? _ons_ goes with a singular het-word (_ons huis_), _onze_ with everything else (_onze auto_, _onze kinderen_). All other possessives never change.

An adjective after a possessive always takes **-e**: _mijn nieuwe fiets_, _ons oude huis_.

### Other ways to show possession

- **van** + person: _de fiets van Anna_, _die jas is van mij_ (that coat is mine).
- **-s** after a name: _Pieters fiets_, _Ellens verjaardag_ (no apostrophe). A name ending in a, i, o, u or y gets **'s**: _Anna's fiets_. A name ending in an s-sound (s, x, z) gets only an apostrophe: _Kees' fiets_, _Max' jas_.
- In speech: name + **z'n / d'r**: _Pieter z'n fiets_, _Anna d'r jas_. Common but informal.
- **de/het mijne** stands without a noun ("mine"): _Is dit jouw pen of de mijne?_ Use _de_ or _het_ to match the noun. In speech, _van mij_ is more usual.

### Pattern

- my: `mijn` (_m'n_)
- your (informal): `jouw` (_je_)
- your (polite): `uw`
- his / its: `zijn` (_z'n_)
- her: `haar` (_d'r_)
- our: `ons` + singular het-word, `onze` + the rest
- your (plural): `jullie` (_je_)
- their: `hun`

## Examples

- Dat is **mijn** jas. | That is my coat.
- Is dit **jouw** telefoon? | Is this your phone?
- **Onze** buren hebben een hond. | Our neighbours have a dog.
- Wij verkopen **ons** huis. | We are selling our house.
- De kinderen spelen met **hun** vrienden. | The children are playing with their friends.
- Deze tas is niet van mij, maar van **mijn** zus. | This bag is not mine but my sister's.

## Common mistakes

- _Onze huis_. Huis is a het-word: _ons huis_.
- _Hun_ for "they" as a subject: _Hun hebben honger._ This is heard but seen as wrong. Say _Zij hebben honger._
- An apostrophe after every name: _Pieter's fiets_. Write _Pieters fiets_; the apostrophe is only for names ending in a vowel (_Anna's_) or an s-sound (_Kees'_).
- No -e after a possessive: _mijn nieuw fiets_. Say _mijn nieuwe fiets_.
