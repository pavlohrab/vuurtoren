# Stories

Graded Dutch stories that ship with Vuurtoren, from A1 to B2. Each level mixes Dutch legends and
folk tales, retold public-domain classics and new stories about life in the Netherlands.

| Level | Length (words) | Words within the starter list | Longest sentence |
| ----- | -------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------- |
| A1    | 150–250        | 95% within the first 1000     | 10 words         |
| A2    | 300–500        | 95% within the first 2000     | 14 words         |
| B1    | 600–900        | 95% within the first 3500     | 20 words         |
| B2    | 900–1400       | 95% within the whole list     | no limit         |

## Covers

`covers/<slug>.webp` is a story's cover, 480×600: old paintings, prints and photos from
Wikimedia Commons. Each one is public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, and `covers/credits.json`
says where it comes from. The reader shows that credit under the story. A story without a file
keeps the plain cover.
