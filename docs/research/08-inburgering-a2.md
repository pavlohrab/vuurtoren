## 1. Who takes A2, who takes B1

| Group                                                                    | Language level     | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Wet inburgering 2013** (obligation started 1 Jan 2013 – 31 Dec 2021)   | **A2** (or higher) | Lezen, Luisteren, Schrijven, Spreken + KNM. Plus ONA if the obligation started on or after 1 Jan 2015, plus PVT (participatieverklaring) from 1 Oct 2017. Still running under transitional law.                    |
| **Wet inburgering 2021**, B1-route (default, obligation from 1 Jan 2022) | **B1**             | B1 language exams + KNM + MAP + PVT.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Wi2021, B1 not feasible                                                  | **A2**             | The gemeente can lower the language exams to A2 once the learner has had at least **600 hours** of lessons at a school with the Blik op Werk keurmerk.                                                             |
| Wi2021, Z-route (self-reliance route)                                    | none required      | 800 hours of language/KNM lessons (+ 800 participation hours for asylum migrants), PVT, MAP and a final interview; ends with a gemeente certificate, not a diploma. Exams may still be taken and are listed on it. |
| Wi2021, O-route (education route)                                        | B1/B2              | Taalschakeltraject towards Dutch education.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Naturalisation** (IND)                                                 | **A2** today       | The A2 inburgeringsexamen (or a Wi2013/Wi2021 diploma at A2 or higher, or Staatsexamen NT2) satisfies the naturalisation test. Many people take the A2 exam voluntarily for this.                                  |

**Announced changes.** The government decided to raise the naturalisation language requirement
from A2 to **B1** and to lengthen the residence period for naturalisation (to 10 years), through an
amendment of the Rijkswet op het Nederlanderschap; the IND published an implementation assessment
(uitvoeringstoets) of the draft bill in **February 2026**, including transitional rules for people
who applied before the change. **[uncertain]** No entry-into-force date was found on an official
page on 2026-11-18; the bill still needed parliamentary passage and (per the Caribbean countries'
request) about a year's notice. The app says "planned, date not set" and links to IND.

Other 2025–2026 changes found:

- **Spreken A2, 1 March 2025:** the multiple-choice items were removed; the exam now consists only
  of recorded spoken answers (DUO news item on inburgeren.nl).
- **Result times:** Lezen, Luisteren and KNM results usually within 8 weeks; Spreken and Schrijven A2
  currently up to **16 weeks** because of the marking backlog; DUO extends the inburgering term by
  6 months for people waiting for these results.
- **2026:** DUO reported a shortage of exam places for Spreken/Schrijven A2 and extra time for a
  small group (news item, May 2026). **[uncertain]** — seen only as a search snippet.
- ONA courses are disappearing; gemeenten may offer MAP instead for voluntary A2 diplomas.

## 2. Exam components at A2

| Part                                             | Format                                                                                                        | Duration   | Questions / tasks                                    | Marking                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lezen** (reading)                              | computer, multiple choice on short everyday texts                                                             | **65 min** | **[uncertain]** about 25 items                       | computer                                                                                  |
| **Luisteren** (listening)                        | computer, short videos and audio fragments, multiple choice                                                   | **45 min** | **[uncertain]** about 25 items                       | computer                                                                                  |
| **Schrijven** (writing)                          | **pen and paper**, 4 tasks: filling in a form and short free writing / correspondence (note, message, e-mail) | **40 min** | 4 tasks                                              | examiners, with DUO's published assessment models (form; free writing and correspondence) |
| **Spreken** (speaking)                           | computer, answers recorded (no MC since 1 Mar 2025); short situations shown on screen                         | **35 min** | **[uncertain]** about 12–16 prompts in several parts | examiners, DUO's assessment model for speaking                                            |
| **KNM** (Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij) | computer, situation-based multiple choice (often with a picture or short film)                                | **45 min** | **[uncertain]** about 40 items                       | computer                                                                                  |
| ONA / MAP                                        | ONA: portfolio + final interview, or a 64-hour course; MAP replaces it in many gemeenten                      | —          | —                                                    | —                                                                                         |
| PVT                                              | participation statement workshop + signing                                                                    | —          | —                                                    | —                                                                                         |

**Pass marks.** Official pages reached did not state the cut-off scores. Commercial sources
consistently report about 18/25 for Lezen and Luisteren, about 28/40 for KNM and about 65% of the
rubric points for Schrijven. **[uncertain]** The app shows these as _estimates_ ("about 70%"),
never as official.

**Dictionary.** A paper NT2 dictionary is reportedly allowed in Lezen and Schrijven, not in the
others. **[uncertain]**

**Costs (inburgeren.nl, "Hoeveel kost inburgeren?").** €50 per exam (Lezen, Luisteren, Schrijven,
Spreken, KNM), ONA €40; full A2 exam €250 (€290 with ONA). Asylum status holders get the first two
attempts of each exam free (not when the exam is taken at a lower level than the PIP says); further
attempts are paid. Retakes are allowed; each attempt is booked and paid separately.

**Booking.** Via _Mijn Inburgering_ on inburgeren.nl with DigiD; DUO runs the exam locations.
Exemptions for parts already passed at B1/B2 or with an NT2 diploma are possible.

## 4. KNM themes

The KNM final attainment levels (eindtermen KNM, Staatscourant 2024, nr. 15802; Besluit
inburgering 2021 art. 3.4) are organised in **eight themes**, each with key actions and indicators:

1. Werk en inkomen (work and income)
2. Omgangsvormen, waarden en normen (manners, values and norms)
3. Wonen (housing)
4. Gezondheid en gezondheidszorg (health and health care)
5. Geschiedenis en geografie (history and geography)
6. Instanties (public bodies: gemeente, Belastingdienst, UWV, DUO, police…)
7. Staatsinrichting en rechtsstaat (system of government and rule of law)
8. Onderwijs en opvoeding (education and upbringing)

**[uncertain]** exact theme titles: taken from the Staatscourant snippet and secondary summaries;
the order and wording may differ slightly in the full text. Kenning's KNM bank uses these eight.
