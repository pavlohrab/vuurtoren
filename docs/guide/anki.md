# Anki

If you already learn Dutch with Anki, you don't have to start over. You can bring your decks into
Vuurtoren, or keep using Anki and read in Vuurtoren next to it.

## Bring your decks in

When you start Vuurtoren and answer **yes** to "Do you use Anki?", you can move your decks into
Vuurtoren with their review history. After that Vuurtoren keeps the cards and runs the reviews, and
you don't need Anki any more.

You can also do it later with a deck file. In Anki, export your deck as `.apkg` (or your whole
collection as `.colpkg`). Then in Vuurtoren open Settings → Your words → **Import a deck file** and
choose the file. Your past reviews, images and sounds come along, and cards you already have stay
as they are. This works on the computer and on the phone.

## Keep using Anki

If you want to keep reviewing in Anki, Vuurtoren can work with it directly. Install the
[AnkiConnect](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2055492159) add-on in Anki (code `2055492159`) and keep
Anki open while you use Vuurtoren. Vuurtoren then reads your decks to know which words you know, and
when you add a word while reading, it goes into your Anki deck.

This works on a computer only for now. On a phone, working with AnkiDroid is planned.

## What Vuurtoren writes to Anki

Vuurtoren reads your decks, but it only writes to Anki when you press a button for a word. Every
note it adds gets a tag, so you can always find it in Anki's browser.

## Going back

You can always move your words back to Anki. Vuurtoren can export your cards as a `.apkg` file
that Anki opens, with their reviews.
