# Anki

You don't have to start over if you already use Anki. Either you bring your decks into Vuurtoren, or
you keep reviewing in Anki and read in Vuurtoren next to it.

## Bring your decks in

Answer **yes** to "Do you use Anki?" at the first start and choose to move your decks. They come in
with their review history, and from then on Vuurtoren runs the reviews.

You can also do it later with a deck file. Export the deck from Anki as `.apkg` (or the whole
collection as `.colpkg`), then in Vuurtoren open Settings → Your words → **Import a deck file**. Past
reviews, images and sounds come along, and cards you already have stay as they are. This works on
the computer and on the phone.

## Keep using Anki

Install the [AnkiConnect](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2055492159) add-on (code `2055492159`) and
keep Anki open. Vuurtoren then reads your decks to know which words you know, and a word you add
while reading goes straight into your Anki deck. It only writes to Anki when you press a button, and
every note it adds is tagged, so you can find it in Anki's browser.

This works on a computer only for now. Working with AnkiDroid on a phone is planned.

## Back to Anki

You can export your Vuurtoren cards as an `.apkg` file, with their reviews, and open it in Anki.
