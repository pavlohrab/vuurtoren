# Your first review

Every day Vuurtoren gives you a short review of your words. The words come back just before you
would forget them, so a few minutes a day is enough.

## Start it

Open **Words**. At the top you see how many words are due today and about how long it takes. Start
the review from there.

## Answer a card

You see the word, and you try to remember what it means. Then show the answer and say how well you
knew it: **Again** if you didn't, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. On a computer you can press the
keys 1 to 4, and Space for Good. If you pressed the wrong one, **Undo** (or Z) takes it back.

On a phone you can just swipe the card: right if you know it, left if you don't, and up if you can
recall it easily. You can swipe before you show the answer too.

## How it decides

Each answer changes when you see the word again. A word you knew easily comes back after a longer
time, and a word you forgot comes back soon. Vuurtoren uses FSRS for this, the algorithm Anki also
offers, and over time it adapts to your own memory.

Reading counts as well. When you read a word without tapping it, its next review waits a little
longer, and when you had to look it up, it comes back today.

## New words

Next to your reviews, a few new words join every day, ten unless you change it. They're the words
you added while reading first, and then the next common words of your level. You can change the
number in Settings → Your words → New words a day. See [Your words](words.md) for more.
