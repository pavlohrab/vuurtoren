# Sound

Vuurtoren can say the words and the sentences out loud, so you hear how they're pronounced.

## The recordings

The about 5000 common words, their example sentences and the stories are recorded in two voices,
a female and a male one. You can choose the voice in Settings → Reading → **Voice**. Press the
speaker next to a word or a sentence to hear it.

On a computer, the desktop app downloads the recordings by itself, together with the rest of the
language data, when it's online. The offline installer has them inside already.

## Words without a recording

Words and sentences that aren't recorded, for example from your own texts, are read by your
device's own Dutch voice. Windows and macOS can add a Dutch voice in their speech settings, and most
phones have one.

On a phone without your own server, the phone's own voice reads everything, because the recordings
come with the language data. With your own server, the phone plays the recordings from there.

## The speaker is crossed out

That means there is no voice for this word on your device. It happens mostly on Linux, where many
systems don't have a Dutch voice. Install one in your system's speech settings, and the words get a
voice.
