# Your words

Every word you learn becomes a card. A card has the Dutch word on the front and its meaning and an
example sentence on the back. Every day a short review brings back the cards you are about to
forget.

## The daily review

Open **Words**. At the top you see how many cards are waiting for you today, and about how long
they take. In the review you see a word, try to remember what it means, and then show the answer.
Then say how well you knew it: **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. On a phone you can swipe
instead: right if you knew it, left if you didn't, and up if it was easy. On a computer the keys 1
to 4 do the same.

Your answer decides when you see the card again. A word you knew easily comes back after a long
time, and a word you forgot comes back soon. Reading counts too: a word you read without needing
help waits a little longer, and a word you had to look up comes back today.

## New words

Ten new words join your review every day. You can change that in **Settings → Your words → New
words a day**. The words you added while reading come first.

If you don't use your own decks, Vuurtoren gives you about 5000 of the most common Dutch words, each
with an example sentence, and starts with the most common ones.

If you were away for a while, you don't come back to a mountain of cards: you get at most 200
reviews a day, and the rest waits for the next days.

## Practice and the exam

**Practice** has short lessons where you use your words in sentences. See
[Practice](practice.md).

**Exam** helps you prepare for the A2 inburgeringsexamen. It has practice tests for reading,
listening, writing, speaking and KNM. If you add your own AI key, it also gives feedback on your
writing and speaking.

## All your words

Below the review is the list of all your words. You can search it and show only new words, words
you are learning, or words you know. Tap a word to see its meaning, its sentences and how your
reviews went.

If you already know a word, press **I know it**, here or while reading, and it leaves your review.
If you pressed it by mistake, press **Undo**, or later **Learn it again** on the word's card.
