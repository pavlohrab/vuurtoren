# Your words

Every word you learn gets a card, and every day a short review brings back the cards you're about
to forget.

## The daily review

Open **Words**. At the top it says how many cards are due and about how long they take. In the
review you see the word, try to remember it, show the answer and say how well you knew it:
**Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. On a computer you can use the keys 1 to 4, Space for Good and Z
to undo. On a phone you swipe right if you know it, left if you don't, and up if it was easy. You
can swipe before you show the answer too.

Your answer decides when the card comes back, with FSRS, the algorithm Anki also offers. A word you
knew easily comes back after a long time, a word you forgot comes back soon, and the schedule adapts
to your own memory over time. Reading counts too: a word you read without tapping it waits a little
longer, and a word you had to look up comes back today.

## New words

Ten new words join your reviews every day, unless you change it in Settings → Your words → **New
words a day**. The words you added while reading come first, then the next common words of your
level. They come in between your reviews, so you don't get them all at the end.

You get at most 200 reviews a day. If you were away for a week, the rest waits for the next days,
so you don't come back to a mountain of cards. You can change it in Settings → Your words →
**Reviews a day**.

If you don't bring your own decks, Vuurtoren gives you about 5000 of the most common Dutch words, in
levels of 500, each with an example sentence. New words start with the most common ones.

## Practice and the exam

**Practice** is short lessons with the words you're learning: building sentences, de or het, the
right form, hearing and saying them. It picks the exercise for each word from what it knows about
you. How it works is on the [Practice](practice.md) page.

**Exam** is for the A2 inburgeringsexamen. It explains the parts of the exam, and has practice tests
for reading, listening, writing, speaking and KNM. With your own AI key you also get feedback on
your writing and speaking, the way an examiner would give it.

## Your list

Below the review are all your words, as cards or as a list, with filters for new, learning and known
and a search. On a computer the list has your folders on the left (To decide, Due, Learning, Known,
your levels and your texts) and the chosen word on the right, where **A** adds a new word and **K**
marks it known. Tap a word to see its meaning, its sentences and how your reviews went. Words you
already know you can mark with **I know it**, here or while reading, and they leave your reviews.
Pressed it by mistake? Use **Undo**, or later **Learn it again** on the word's card.
