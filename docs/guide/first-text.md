# Your first text

You can read any Dutch text in Vuurtoren, at your level. The words you don't know yet get a short
translation above them, and a few of them are picked for you to learn.

## Add it

Open **Read** and press **Add text**. Paste your text in the box, for example a news article, a
message from school or a page you copied from a book, and press **Add to my texts**. Vuurtoren goes
through the text for a few seconds and then opens it.

If you'd rather start with something ready, the stories on **Read** are already prepared and fit
your level, so you can just open one.

## Read it

The text opens one page at a time. Most words are plain, a few new words are underlined, and the
words you don't know get their English above them. Tap any word to see what it means in this
sentence. If you want to learn it, press **Add to my words** (or **Add to Anki** if you use Anki),
and it joins your reviews.

If a word is one you already know, press **I know it**, and Vuurtoren stops translating it. For
names and words you don't care about, press **Ignore**.

## Good to know

The text stays on your device, and nobody else sees it. The more words you know, the fewer
translations you see, and you can turn them up or down in the reading settings.

## If it doesn't work

On a phone, a new text waits until it's prepared, because preparing a text needs your computer or
your own server. Add it on your computer and sync, or connect your server in Settings → Your
devices. See [On your phone](../phones.md).

If the text looks broken, you probably copied the menus of the web page too. Copy only the text
itself, or give the web address instead (see [Add texts](add-texts.md)).
