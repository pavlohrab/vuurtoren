# Add texts

You can read almost anything in Vuurtoren. Open **Read**, press **Add text**, and choose where the
text comes from.

## Paste

Copy a Dutch text from anywhere and paste it in the box. Give it a title if you like, otherwise
Vuurtoren takes one from the text.

## A web page

Choose **Web** and paste the address of the page, for example a news article. Vuurtoren takes only
the article text from the page, without the menus and adverts.

## A PDF or a photo

Choose **PDF** or **Photo** and pick the file, or on a phone take a photo of the page. Vuurtoren
reads the text from it. Because reading a photo isn't always perfect, you then get a short check:
words it wasn't sure about are marked, and you can accept its suggestions or fix them yourself
before the text is added.

Scanned pages need text recognition, which runs on your computer or your own server. On a phone
without a server, add them on your computer.

## A story written for you

With your own AI key, **Write a story** makes a new text at your level, about a topic you choose,
with a few new words in it. See [Add your AI](../ai.md).

## A whole course book

If you follow a textbook, open **Courses** and press **Add a course**. You can add the book as a PDF
or an EPUB, as photos of the pages, or as a list of web pages. Vuurtoren splits it into units, you
check the outline, and then you read and practise the book unit by unit.

## On a phone

A text you add on a phone has to be prepared first, and that needs your computer or your own
server. Until then it waits. Add it on your computer and sync, or connect your server in Settings →
Your devices. See [On your phone](../phones.md).
