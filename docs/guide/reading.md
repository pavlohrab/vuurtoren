# Reading

You can read any Dutch text in Vuurtoren. In every part of about 150 words, it marks about three
new words for you to learn. You can tap any other word to see what it means.

## Add a text

Open **Read** and press **Add text**. You can paste a text, give the address of a web page, or
choose a PDF or a photo. From a web page Vuurtoren takes only the article. When you add a photo, you
check the words the app couldn't read well before the text is added. If you add your own AI key,
you can also press **Write a story** to get a new story at your level about a topic you choose.

**Read** also has 35 ready stories, from A1 to B2. A textbook goes in **Courses → Add a course**,
and you read it unit by unit.

A phone can't prepare a new text by itself. It needs your computer or a server for that, see
[On your phone](../phones.md).

## The new words

The new words are marked with a soft colour and a line under them. They are not translated, so you
can first try to guess them from the sentence. If you look up only a few words, the next parts get
one more new word. If you look up a lot, they get one fewer. You can also choose the number
yourself in the reading settings, the **Aa** button above the text.

Tap a word to see what it means in this sentence. **Add to my words** adds it to your review, and
**I know it** tells Vuurtoren you already know it.

## Turning pages

On a phone you turn pages like a book: swipe, or tap the left or right side of the page. On a
computer you see two pages side by side. Tap the middle of the page to hide the bars around it. If
you prefer to scroll, choose that under **Aa → Pages**.

## Four ways to read

You choose how you read at the top of the page.

- **Read** shows the text with the new words marked.
- **Gloss** shows the English next to the words you may not know, so you can read without
  stopping.
- **Cloze** hides some words you are learning, and you fill them in.
- **Side by side** shows each sentence with its English. It scrolls, also on a phone.

**Focus mode** (⤢, or the F key) shows only the text, on the whole screen. Esc takes you back.

## Grammar

Some grammar is marked with a line next to the sentence, like a verb that splits in two. Tap the
line to see what it is. Every mark links to its topic in **Grammar**, which has 63 topics from A1
to C1.

## Sound

The common words, their sentences and the stories are recorded by a real person. You can choose a
female or a male voice in **Settings → Reading → Voice**. Other words are read by your computer's
or phone's own Dutch voice. Linux computers often don't have one, so there Vuurtoren uses its own.

On a phone the recordings are a download of about 230 MB, in the same place in Settings. The app
starts it by itself on Wi-Fi. Until then the phone's voice reads everything. If the speaker icon is
crossed out, your phone has no Dutch voice, and you can add one in the phone's speech settings.
