# Quick start

From nothing to reading your first Dutch text takes about five minutes.

1. **Install Vuurtoren.** Download it from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/pavlohrab/Vuurtoren/releases/latest): the `.exe` on Windows,
   the `.dmg` on a Mac, the `.AppImage` or `.deb` on Linux, or `Vuurtoren-android.apk` on your
   phone. The app isn't signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time. How to get past
   that is on [Install](install.md).
2. **Answer a few questions.** Vuurtoren asks whether you use Anki. If you don't, it keeps your
   words itself and starts with the most common ones. If you do, see [Anki](anki.md).
3. **Read a story.** **Read** opens with a story at your level. About three new words per page are
   marked for you to learn. Tap any word to see what it means, and press **Add to my words** to
   learn it. If you want English above the words you may not know, switch the page to **Gloss**.

   <figure class="clip-fig"><video class="clip" src="../clips/tap-a-word.webm" poster="../clips/tap-a-word.png" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Tap a word: what it means in this sentence, and its grammar."></video><figcaption>Tap a word: what it means in this sentence, and its grammar.</figcaption></figure>

4. **Add your own text.** Press **Add text** on **Read** and paste a Dutch text, give a web
   address, or choose a PDF or a photo. On a phone, a new text needs your computer or your own
   server first, see [On your phone](../phones.md).
5. **Do your first review.** Open **Words** and start the review. Say how well you knew each word,
   or on a phone swipe right if you know it and left if you don't. A few minutes a day is enough.
   **Practice** under **Words** then has short lessons with the same words.

   <figure class="clip-fig"><video class="clip" src="../clips/practise.webm" poster="../clips/practise.png" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="A lesson in Practice: choose, and the band at the bottom says if it was right."></video><figcaption>A lesson in Practice: choose, and the band at the bottom says if it was right.</figcaption></figure>

The first time you open a story, a short tour shows you around: tapping a word, its card, the
sentence and its grammar, **Aa**, and the ways to read. You can skip it at any step, and start it
again from **Settings → Reading** or, on a phone, from **More**.

When you want your words on more devices, see [Sync your devices](sync.md).

## Telling Pavlo what you think

Vuurtoren is in beta, and Pavlo reads everything you send. On a computer press **Send feedback** in
the sidebar (or Ctrl+Shift+F). On a phone use the **Feedback** tab on the side of the screen, or
shake the phone. You can also find it in **Settings → Data**, and every error message has **Tell
Pavlo**.

Write what happened, and if it helps, add the screen (you can draw on it to point at something) or
a short voice note. The app version and your device go with it, and the last errors if you leave
them on. When Pavlo answers, you see it in the same place, like a chat. On a phone you can hide the
side tab in **Settings → Data → Feedback**.
