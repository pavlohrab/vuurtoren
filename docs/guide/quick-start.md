# Quick start

From nothing to reading your first Dutch text takes about five minutes.

1. **Install Vuurtoren.** Download it from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/pavlohrab/Vuurtoren/releases/latest): the `.exe` on Windows,
   the `.dmg` on a Mac, the `.AppImage` or `.deb` on Linux, or `Vuurtoren-android.apk` on your
   phone. The app isn't signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time. How to get past
   that is on [Install](install.md).
2. **Answer a few questions.** Vuurtoren asks if you use Anki, a flashcard app. If you don't,
   Vuurtoren keeps your words itself and starts with the most common Dutch words. If you do, see
   [Anki](anki.md).
3. **Read a story.** **Read** opens a story at your level. On each page about three new words are
   marked for you to learn. Tap any word to see what it means here, and press **Add to my words**
   to learn it.

   <figure class="clip-fig"><video class="clip" src="../clips/tap-a-word.webm" poster="../clips/tap-a-word.png" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Tap a word to see what it means in this sentence."></video><figcaption>Tap a word to see what it means in this sentence.</figcaption></figure>

4. **Add your own text.** On **Read**, press **Add text** and paste a Dutch text, give a web
   address, or choose a PDF or a photo. A phone can't prepare a new text by itself: it needs your
   computer or a server, see [On your phone](../phones.md).
5. **Do your first review.** Every word you learn becomes a card, and a short review every day
   brings back the cards you are about to forget. Open **Words** and start the review. A few
   minutes a day is enough. **Practice** has short lessons with the same words.

   <figure class="clip-fig"><video class="clip" src="../clips/practise.webm" poster="../clips/practise.png" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="A lesson in Practice: choose an answer and see if it was right."></video><figcaption>A lesson in Practice: choose an answer and see if it was right.</figcaption></figure>

The first time you open a story, a short tour shows you around. You can start it again from
**Settings → Reading**.

When you want your words on more devices, see [Sync your devices](sync.md).

## Telling Pavlo what you think

Vuurtoren is in beta. To tell me what you think or what broke, use **Send feedback** in the
sidebar, or the **Feedback** tab on a phone. My answers come back in the same place.
