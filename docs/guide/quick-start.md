# Quick start

From nothing to reading your first Dutch text takes about five minutes.

1. **Install Vuurtoren.** Download it from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/pavlohrab/Vuurtoren/releases/latest): the `.exe` on Windows,
   the `.dmg` on a Mac, the `.AppImage` or `.deb` on Linux, or `Vuurtoren-android.apk` on your
   phone. The app isn't signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time. How to get past
   that is on [Install](install.md).
2. **Answer a few questions.** Vuurtoren asks whether you use Anki. If you don't, it keeps your
   words itself and starts with the most common ones. If you do, see [Anki](anki.md).
3. **Read a story.** **Read** opens with a story at your level. About three new words per page are
   marked for you to learn. Tap any word to see what it means, and press **Add to my words** to
   learn it. If you want English above the words you may not know, switch the page to **Gloss**.
4. **Add your own text.** Press **Add text** on **Read** and paste a Dutch text, give a web
   address, or choose a PDF or a photo. On a phone, a new text needs your computer or your own
   server first, see [On your phone](../phones.md).
5. **Do your first review.** Open **Words** and start the review. Say how well you knew each word,
   or on a phone swipe right if you know it and left if you don't. A few minutes a day is enough.

When you want your words on more devices, see [Sync your devices](sync.md).
