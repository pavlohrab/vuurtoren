How it works

It measures what you know.

Vuurtoren knows which words you know. It uses that to give each page a few new words to learn, and to help you with the rest.

Every page

Just enough help on every page.

Each page has about three new words for you to learn. Vuurtoren picks common words you don't know yet1, and words that come back later in the text, so you meet them again6. If another word stops you, tap it, or switch to Gloss to see the English above it2.

You can follow a text when you know about 95% of its words, and read it with ease at 98%3–5.

Your words

It knows which words you know.

Every review tells Vuurtoren a bit more about what you remember. It uses FSRS, the same algorithm as Anki1–4, and the words you read count too.

Offline

It runs on your computer.

Tap a word to see what it means and how its sentence is built. All of this is worked out on your computer with open tools and dictionaries, so it works offline and your texts stay with you.

spaCy · SyntaxDot (de Kok et al. 2020) · OPUS-MT · Wiktionary via Kaikki · Tatoeba · wordfreq

Optional · AI

How the AI stories are made.

With your own AI key, Vuurtoren can write a story for you. It tells the AI which words you know, asks for only a few new ones, and checks the story before you read it.

How it works in detail →
  • Your wordsknown and learning words go to the writer
  • A few newabout 3% of the words, each used more than once
  • Checkedagainst your words before you read it

AI is there if you want it.

With your own key, it can also explain a sentence or talk with you in Dutch. You don't need it for anything else.
