Your data, always yours.

Everything lives on your devices and works offline. No account. Export your cards to Anki at any time; the code is open for anyone to read.

Sync seamlessly.

Start a story on your computer, finish it on your phone. Sync through your own server, WebDAV or a Syncthing folder, locked with your passphrase. Vuurtoren Sync: planned.

Install

Three steps on Android.

1 · DownloadOpen the latest release on your phone and take the .apk file.
2 · InstallOpen the file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it.
3 · ConnectIn the app, Settings → Your devices: join your computer's sync data with your passphrase.
