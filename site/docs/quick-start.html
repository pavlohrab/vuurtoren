Quick start

From nothing to reading your first Dutch text takes about five minutes.

  1. Install Vuurtoren. Download it from the latest release: the .exe on Windows, the .dmg on a Mac, the .AppImage or .deb on Linux, or Vuurtoren-android.apk on your phone. The app isn't signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time. How to get past that is on Install.

  2. Answer a few questions. Vuurtoren asks whether you use Anki. If you don't, it keeps your words itself and starts with the most common ones. If you do, see Anki.

  3. Read a story. Read opens with a story at your level. About three new words per page are marked for you to learn. Tap any word to see what it means, and press Add to my words to learn it. If you want English above the words you may not know, switch the page to Gloss.

  4. Add your own text. Press Add text on Read and paste a Dutch text, give a web address, or choose a PDF or a photo. On a phone, a new text needs your computer or your own server first, see On your phone.

  5. Do your first review. Open Words and start the review. Say how well you knew each word, or on a phone swipe right if you know it and left if you don't. A few minutes a day is enough. Practice under Words then has short lessons with the same words.

The first time you open a story, a short tour shows you around. You can start it again from Settings → Reading.

When you want your words on more devices, see Sync your devices.

Telling Pavlo what you think

Vuurtoren is in beta. To tell me what you think or what broke, use Send feedback in the sidebar, or the Feedback tab on a phone. My answers come back in the same place.
