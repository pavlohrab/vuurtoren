Sync your devices

Sync keeps your words, texts, reviews and progress the same on all your devices. Every device keeps its own full copy, and the sync place only passes the changes between them.

Is it safe?

Yes. Before anything leaves your device, Vuurtoren locks it with your passphrase, so the sync place only ever sees locked files that it can't read. When you set up sync, you also get a recovery key. Keep it somewhere safe, because it opens your data if you forget the passphrase.

Where your data can sync through

All your devices have to use the same place. There are three options for now.

Place Devices Good for
Your own Vuurtoren server computer and phone You self-host a server
WebDAV computer and phone Nextcloud, ownCloud, a NAS
A folder you already sync computer (desktop app) Syncthing, Dropbox, iCloud Drive

Vuurtoren Sync (sync.vuurtoren.app) is planned. Then you'll just sign in, and your devices sync without setting anything up.

Set it up

Everything happens in Settings → Your devices.

On your first device, which is a computer or a phone (never the server):

  1. Choose the place and fill in its details (see below).
  2. Choose Start new sync data here and type a passphrase. A few words you'll remember work well.
  3. Save the recovery key it shows you.

On every other device:

  1. Choose the same place.
  2. Choose Join my synced data and type the same passphrase. Your data then downloads.

Your own server

You can connect a device by typing the server's address, your user name and your password. The device gets its own access, and the password itself is not kept.

It's easier with a code. Open the server's address in a browser, sign in and press Add a device. On a phone you scan the QR code, and on a computer you paste the link under it. The code works once, for 10 minutes, and it never holds your passphrase. You can remove a device again on the server's Your devices page.

A phone that syncs with your server also uses it as its language server, so it can prepare the texts you add on the phone. The desktop app doesn't need that, because it has its own.

WebDAV (Nextcloud)

  1. In Nextcloud Files, make a folder called Vuurtoren.
  2. Copy the WebDAV address from Files → Files settings, and add /Vuurtoren at the end.
  3. Make an app password in Settings → Security → Devices & sessions → Create new app password.
  4. In Vuurtoren, choose WebDAV and fill in the address, your user name and the app password.

A synced folder (Syncthing)

  1. Make a folder, for example ~/Sync/Vuurtoren, and share it in Syncthing with your other computers.
  2. In Vuurtoren on each computer, choose A folder you already sync and pick that folder. Don't pick the folder where Vuurtoren keeps its own data.

Phones can't share a folder like this, so on a phone use your server or WebDAV.

When two devices change the same thing

Every change is saved with a time, and every device puts all the changes in the same order, so in the end they all show the same thing. For settings, word marks and your profile, the latest change wins. Reviews and answers are never lost: if you review on two devices, both count. Words and texts added on two devices are both kept, and the same word added twice is one word.

How fast

While Vuurtoren is open on two devices, a change on one shows up on the other within about 5 to 20 seconds. It syncs when it opens, a few seconds after a change, every 15 seconds and when you come back to it, and it sends what's left when you close it.

Good to know

You can change your passphrase in Settings → Your devices, and your other devices keep syncing. If you lose your passphrase, type the recovery key where Vuurtoren asks for the passphrase. To stop syncing on one device, use Settings → Your devices; its data stays on it.

If you lose a device, remove it on your server's page. To be safe, start new sync data with a new passphrase and join it again from your other devices.

And keep a backup now and then, in Settings → Data → Download backup.
