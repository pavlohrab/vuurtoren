Why I made it

While learning Dutch, I ran into a problem: there were hardly any texts suited to a beginner reader. On top of that, learning the words from them was tedious.

So Vuurtoren adapts any text to the words you know, and one tap turns a new word into a flashcard with the sentence you found it in. Vuurtoren is Dutch for lighthouse. A lighthouse helps you find your way through unfamiliar water, and that is what I want this app to do for a text.

Reading

Four ways to read the same page.

Every text adapts to what you know. Choose how much help you want, and change it on any page.

  • Stories from A1 to B2, recorded in two voices.
  • Your own texts: a web page, a PDF, a photo of a book.
  • Tap a word for its meaning in that sentence and its grammar.

Words

The words you meet come back.

One tap keeps a word with the sentence you found it in. A few minutes a day brings it back just before you would forget it.

  • Scheduled with FSRS, the same algorithm Anki uses.
  • The 5000 most common Dutch words, from the level you choose.
  • Hear the word, then its sentence. Swipe or tap to answer.
  • Your Anki decks come in and go out as .apkg files.
Open, and yours

No account. Your words stay with you.

Everything lives on your devices and works offline. Sync is locked with your passphrase before it leaves, and the code is open for anyone to read.

  • Free and open source (MIT).
  • AI only if you want it, with your own key.
