The name

Why a lighthouse?

Vuurtoren is Dutch for lighthouse: vuur, fire, on a toren, a tower.

The name is a metaphor: the app doesn't take you through the dark waters of Dutch, but it lights up the path and makes it easier to find your way.

Grammar

Learn the grammar the proper way.

Vuurtoren has 63 grammar topics, from A1 to C1. The grammar is highlighted in the texts you read and explained under each word.

The rules follow the e-ANS, the reference grammar of Dutch, and Taaladvies, simplified for learners. Based on Donaldson 2008 and Shetter & Ham 2007.

Elke avond beklimt Jan de trap. Hij zet het licht aan.

Inversion The sentence starts with "Elke avond", so the verb comes before "Jan". Separable verb "zet … aan" is aanzetten, to switch on.

Why I made it

While learning Dutch, I ran into a problem: there were hardly any texts suited to a beginner reader. On top of that, learning the words from them was tedious.

So I made the app I wanted: one that adapts any text to the words you know, and turns a new word into a flashcard with the sentence you found it in. It is free and open.
