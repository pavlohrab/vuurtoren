Reading

One page, four ways to read it.

ReadThe text as it is. A few new words a page are marked to learn, and grammar is underlined.
GlossA short translation above the words you don't know yet. A dial sets how many.
ClozeThe words you are learning become gaps, with a hint. Fill them as you read.
Side by sideEach sentence next to its English, word paired with word.

Words

The words you meet come back.

One click keeps a word with the sentence you found it in. Reviews use FSRS, the scheduler Anki uses: a few minutes a day, with the keys 1 to 4.

Anki

Bring your decks, or keep Anki.

Import your .apkg files and Vuurtoren knows your level from the first day. Or keep reviewing in Anki: with the AnkiConnect add-on, Vuurtoren reads your progress and adds new words to your deck.

Grammar

Grammar, where it happens.

Inversion, separable verbs and the perfect tense are marked in your own texts. Lessons from A1 to C1 explain them on your sentences, and mock exams prepare the A2 inburgeringsexamen.

Everything lives on your devices and works offline. No account. Export your cards to Anki at any time; the code is open for anyone to read.

Sync seamlessly.
